Here's the framework we follow:

Step 1: Inventory

Know what you're working with.

The Money Momentum Inventory™ comes first: a deep dive into your assets, experience, and vision. You complete it before we meet, and I go through it ahead of time, so we don't spend our half day on the basics. (Nothing gets designed until we know what's already there.)

Step 2: Innovate

Design what's next. Grounded in what's real.

A half-day private Strategic Innovation Intensive, at my home in NYC or on Zoom. We work from your Inventory — your assets, experience, relationships, and hard-won insight — to shape your next move. This is where we design or redesign your offers, positioning, and strategy so they fit both the revenue and what you want from the business. Built on what's real and viable, not theoretical. We also decide what stays and what's ready to go.

Your 90 days start after the intensive.

Step 3: Implement

Make it real. Bring it to market.

We execute your marketing and sales strategy — creating steady, sustainable demand for what you've built. This is where the work moves from design into the world, with systems and messaging that generate revenue without starting from scratch every time you need to sell.

Step 4: Optimize

Refine what's working. Release what's not.

As you implement, we calibrate — adjusting tactics, tightening messaging, and protecting your time and energy. We're looking for the balance of profit, impact, and ease where the business serves your life, not the other way around.
